# Code of Conduct

Codex OSS Lens is a small public project for maintainers who want privacy-preserving Codex usage
evidence. Project discussions should stay practical, respectful, and focused on improving the tool.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear, constructive, and specific when reporting bugs or proposing changes.
- Respect maintainer time by including reproduction steps, versions, and share-safe examples.
- Keep feedback focused on code, documentation, workflows, and public evidence quality.
- Use `SUPPORT.md` for support routing and `SECURITY.md` for vulnerability or sensitive-data reports.
- Preserve the privacy boundary: do not post raw Codex logs, private prompts, credentials, tokens,
  private source code, or full local filesystem paths in public project spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Posting another person's private information or private repository content.
- Repeated off-topic requests after a maintainer has redirected the discussion.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose private Codex sessions, prompts, credentials, or local paths.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this policy. Repeated or
severe violations may result in blocked participation in the repository.

If a conduct concern also involves sensitive security or privacy details, do not post the details
publicly. Follow `SECURITY.md` first, then use `SUPPORT.md` to ask for an appropriate private
contact path.
